STRUCTURED QR PERMISSION SYSTEM

Your number is private.
Your permission is revocable.

Issue an individual QR access credential, limit calling to specific days and hours, and revoke printed paper instantly. Callers contact your existing phone through a privacy gateway; they do not receive your phone number.

Designed for modern mobile browsers and installable as a web app. Calling needs an internet connection and an authorized telecommunications relay.

QRACCESS ≠ NUMBER

01 / GATED DELIVERY

One-time activation · Defined lifetime upgrades

Complete the one-time support purchase through a verified Stripe Checkout. After payment confirmation, a single-use QR is issued for the source/release package. QR previews do not consume it.

The payment gateway is not configured on this public page. No payment can be collected here until it is connected.

Lifetime upgrades apply to the named QR Call core product while it is maintained. Third-party calling minutes, SMS, infrastructure, and major separate products are excluded; see the terms.

02 / VERIFIED PHONE ACTIVATION

SMS verification before operation

The owner's QR operations remain locked until the registered destination telephone successfully passes an independent SMS ownership challenge. No code in a QR can bypass phone permissions or execute shell commands.

03 / GLYPHMATICS

Authenticated encryption, not security by obscurity

GlyphMatics GM1 envelopes protect sensitive stored values using AES-256-GCM with HKDF-SHA-256 key derivation. The platform checks permission state, expiry, schedule, and revocation before connecting a call.

COMMAND POLICY

A QR is a grant of permission, never executable software

📞 Call

A one-person calling link with date boundaries, recurring access hours, quota and instant revocation.

📦 Download

A payment-confirmed delivery link expires after 30 minutes, and one successful redemption consumes it.

🔐 Activate

Registered phone confirmation is required; the browser cannot simply read your telephone number without permission.

🛑 Revoke

Revoke one printed credential without changing your number or disrupting other printed credentials.
